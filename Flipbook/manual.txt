================================================================================
  FLIPBOOK PDF READER
  Version 1.8.23  -  Released 2026-11-15
================================================================================

Flipbook reads any PDF like a real book: two pages side by side, turned with a
3D page-flip animation. It builds a table of contents for every book, lets you
add your own bookmarks, remembers where you stopped reading, can read the book
aloud to you (with an optional natural, human-sounding AI voice), and can look
up the meaning and origin of any word you click.

It runs entirely on your computer and works offline. Nothing is uploaded.

New in 1.8.23:
  - Contents pages: a page number that text recognition misread as letters
    (such as "il" for 1) no longer sends you to the next entry's page.
    Flipbook works out the number and goes to the page with that title
    (section 5, "Page links").
  - 1.8.22: the free Black's 2nd edition download keeps trying when one of
    archive.org's servers has an expired security certificate, with a small
    note in the download window (section 10).
  - 1.8.21 regrouped the dictionaries in "🧩 Add-ons" into two boxes,
    "Etymology dictionary" and "Legal dictionary", and added a warning when
    a dictionary looks like the wrong kind for its box (section 10).

For every earlier version, see "ⓘ About" > "Version history".

  1. WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Flipbook.exe     The application. It is the only file needed to run.
                   Everything it uses (reader engine, text recognition,
                   read-aloud engine, fonts, this manual) is built into
                   this one file.
  Flipbook - SHA-256 checksum.txt
                   The checksum and exact size of Flipbook.exe, and how to
                   check that your copy is the genuine, undamaged file.
                   (Not needed to run Flipbook.)

  Created automatically while you use it:
  flipbook-data\   Your book list (books.json), bookmarks, each book's table
                   of contents, and your pronunciation fixes.
  addons\voice\        The natural AI voice - only if you download it
                       (about 206 MB, section 9); addons\voice\gpu\ holds
                       its graphics-card speed-up (about 326 MB) if added.
  addons\qwen\         The Qwen3-TTS voices - only if you download them
                       (about 7.4 GB, section 9).
  addons\dictionary\   The free etymology dictionary (Webster's 1913 and
                       WordNet) - only if you download it (about 46 MB
                       once built, section 10).
  addons\legal\        Black's Law Dictionary (the free 2nd edition and
                       your own 4th / 5th) - only if you add it (section 10).
  addons\mydicts\      Dictionaries you added from your own PDFs - only
                       if you add one (section 10).

  Optional:
  *.pdf            Any PDF placed in this folder appears in the Library.
                   (Books anywhere else join the Library when you open or
                   add them.)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  2. SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit.
  - Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime. It is already part of Windows 11 and of
    up-to-date Windows 10. If it is missing, Flipbook tells you so on start.
    Since 1.8.7 the PDF engine needs a recent version (it normally updates
    itself). If it is too old, the Home screen says so and books won't
    open until it is updated from the link below.
    Download (free): https://developer.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/webview2/
  - No Python or other software is needed. An internet connection is only
    needed once per add-on, if you choose to download one.
  - Disk: about 66 MB for the app, plus your PDFs, plus 206 MB for the AI
    voice (+326 MB for its graphics-card speed-up), 46 MB for the free
    etymology dictionary and about 7.4 GB for the Qwen3-TTS voices, if you add them.
  - Graphics card (optional): any graphics card with at least 2 GB of its
    own video memory can speed up the AI voice. The Qwen3-TTS voices need an
    NVIDIA graphics card with about 6 GB of video memory or more.
  - Memory: 4 GB or more is recommended for very large PDFs (thousands of
    pages) and for the AI voice.
  - Speakers or headphones for Read aloud.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  3. GETTING STARTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Put this folder wherever you like (for example Documents\Flipbook).
     Choose a folder you can write to - not "Program Files".
  2. Double-click Flipbook.exe.
  3. The first time on a new PC, Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows
     protected your PC" because the app is not code-signed. Click
     "More info" and then "Run anyway". This happens only once.
  4. The Home screen appears ("Welcome to Flipbook"). Click "📂 Open a
     PDF..." to open a book from anywhere on your PC. From then on, each time
     Flipbook starts, Home says "Welcome back" and shows the book you read
     last, with a picture of the page where you stopped and a "Continue
     reading at page N" button (section 4).
     At the bottom of Home: "🧩 Add-ons" (the optional add-ons) and the
     version number.
  5. When a book is open, the tools are in the bar on the right side. The
     first time, a short tip points this out. Click "⌨ Commands" at the
     bottom of that bar (or press ?) to see every key and mouse action, and
     "🧩 Add-ons" to add the natural AI voice or the dictionaries.
  6. To read this manual inside Flipbook, click "ⓘ About" in the tools
     bar, just under "⌨ Commands". Click a line of its Contents to jump to
     that section. Its "Version history" tab lists what changed in each
     version.

  Optional - shortcuts:
  - Desktop: right-click Flipbook.exe > Show more options > Send to >
    Desktop (create shortcut).
  - Start menu / taskbar: right-click Flipbook.exe > Pin to Start, or
    Pin to taskbar.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  4. OPENING PDFs
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Any of these work:
  - "📂 Open a PDF..." on the Home screen (Windows' Open dialog).
  - Pick a book in the Library (see below).
  - Drag a PDF from File Explorer onto the Flipbook window.
  - Drag one or more PDFs (or a folder of PDFs) onto Flipbook.exe itself.
  - Right-click a PDF > Open with > Flipbook. (If Flipbook is not listed,
    choose "Choose another app" > "More apps" > "Look for another app on this
    PC" and select Flipbook.exe.) This does not change your default PDF app.

  Home - the start screen (new in 1.6)
  Each time Flipbook starts, Home shows:
  - "Continue reading": the book you read last, with a picture of the page
    where you stopped, the page number (e.g. "Page 57 of 300"), how long ago,
    and a progress bar. Click "Continue reading at page 57" (or the picture)
    to open it right there.
  - "📂 Open a PDF..." to open a different book, and "📚 Your library" to
    see all your books.
  - At the bottom: "🧩 Add-ons" (the optional AI voices and dictionaries) and
    the version number.
  (Starting Flipbook by opening a PDF - "Open with", or dragging a PDF onto
  Flipbook.exe - opens that PDF directly instead.)

  The Library - your books
  Open it with "📚 Your library" on Home, or the "Library" button at the top
  while reading.
  - Top left: "← Back to book" returns to the book you are reading (or
    "← Home" when no book is open).
  - Down the left side: every book you have opened, newest first, plus any
    you added and every PDF in Flipbook's own folder. Under each name: the
    page you got to and when, or "To read". The open book is highlighted.
  - In the middle: a large picture of the chosen book - the page where you
    stopped, or its first page if you haven't started it - with its details
    and one button: "Continue at page N", "Start reading", or "Back to this
    book".
  - Click a book on the left to see it in the middle. Double-click it (or
    press Enter, or click the page picture, or the button) to open it.
  - "＋ Add PDFs to your library..." (bottom left): choose one or more PDFs
    (hold Ctrl to pick several). They join the list as "To read", ready to
    open whenever you like.
  - Remove: point at a book and click the ✕ that appears, then confirm. This
    only takes the book off the list: the PDF file is NOT deleted, and its
    bookmarks and contents are kept and come back if you open it again.
  - Search: with more than six books, a "Search your books..." box appears.
  - "File not found" means the PDF was moved, renamed or deleted since. Open
    it from its new place (its bookmarks come along) and remove the old
    entry.
  - The list is kept in flipbook-data\books.json, next to Flipbook.exe.
  - Books opened by dragging them into the window or with "Open a PDF..."
    are listed with their full location, so they can be opened again later
    from wherever they are on your PC.

  Each book keeps its own page position, table of contents and bookmarks.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  5. READING AND TURNING PAGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Turn a page: click the arrow at either side, press the arrow keys, scroll
    the mouse wheel, or grab a page corner and drag it across like a real page.
  - Jump: type a page number in the "Page" box in the tools bar and press
    Enter (or click Go), press G, drag the slider at the bottom, or use the
    first/last page buttons.
  - Read up close: double-click a page to open it large. Scroll to read,
    then click or press Esc to close.
  - Full screen: "Full screen" in the tools bar, or press F. The whole
    screen shows only the book: the top bar, Contents and Library, the page
    slider at the bottom, the tools bar, the page-turn arrows and the Read
    aloud bar are all hidden. If CC is on while Read aloud is open, its
    caption box stays at the bottom (the book is fitted above it).
      - A note in the middle says "Full screen - Press Esc to exit" for a
        few seconds.
      - Turn pages with the arrow keys, Page Up / Page Down, Space, a click
        on a page's edge or a drag. R, B, [ and ] still work.
      - Bookmarks: point at the top corner of a page and click the ribbon
        spot. The colour palette opens right there: pick a colour to
        bookmark the page in it, or on a bookmarked page change its colour
        or remove it.
      - Esc (or F) returns to the normal window.
  - In a narrow window, or when the sidebars leave a tall, narrow space,
    Flipbook shows one page at a time; widen the window for two.
  - Flipbook remembers the last page you read in every book and opens there
    next time.
  - The top bar shows the book title in the centre, with the part/chapter
    you are in underneath.

  The tools bar (right side)
    » / «         Icons only / show the names (remembered)
    Page  Go      Go to a page (in icons-only view: #, which asks for the
                  page number)
    🔍 Look up words   Look-up mode on / off (D)       - section 10
      Etymology | ⚖ Legal  Which dictionary Look-up uses: the etymology
                       dictionaries or the legal ones     - section 10
    🔗 Page links      Follow links and contents lines on / off (L)
                       - "Page links" below
    🔊 Read aloud      Show / hide the Read aloud bar (R) - section 8
    Bookmark           Bookmark this page in a colour you choose, or change
                       its colour / remove it (B: bookmark at once in the
                       last colour, or remove)          - section 7
                       Its icon is a small ribbon in the page's colour.
    ☀ / ☾             Light or dark theme (T)          - section 12
    ⛶ Full screen      (F)
    🧩 Add-ons         Add or remove the optional add-ons - sections 9, 10
    ⌨ Commands         Every key and mouse action (?)  - section 13
    ⓘ About            This manual, and the version history in its
                       "Version history" tab
  The names are shown when the window is wide (1280 pixels or more) and
  icons only in smaller windows; click » or « to choose. In very narrow
  windows the bar always shows icons only. Hover over an icon to see its
  name and key.

  Page links (new in 1.8.8)
  Click a line of a book's printed contents page, or a link built into the
  PDF, to go straight to its page.
  - Clicking a page still turns it. Only a click right on a link goes
    somewhere else, and dragging a page always turns it, even if you
    start on a link.
  - Point at a link: the pointer becomes a hand, the line gets a soft
    underline, and a tooltip says where it goes ("Go to page 45 of the
    book: Chapter IV ...").
  - Contents pages: Flipbook recognises a contents page by its column of
    page numbers on the right. The number is the book's printed page
    number, which is often not the page number in the file (the front
    matter, plates and maps add pages). Flipbook finds the right page by
    reading the printed page numbers at the top or bottom of the pages.
    A title that runs onto a second line is one link. A heading with no
    page number of its own ("Chapter IV", "PART TWO") goes to the page of
    the first entry under it.
  - A page number that text recognition misread as letters (for example
    "il" for 1) is worked out from the numbers it could be, between the
    entries above and below it, and Flipbook goes to the page that shows
    the entry's title. If it can't be sure, it says so and stays put; it
    never sends you to the next entry's page.
  - Scanned books: their contents pages are read with text recognition.
    Flipbook does this only for the first pages of a book and the pages
    under a "Contents" entry, a second or two after you turn to them.
    Finding the page then takes a few seconds ("Finding page 45 of the
    book..."). If the page can't be found, Flipbook says so and stays put.
  - Web links built into a PDF are not opened (Flipbook never goes on the
    internet). A click shows their address.
  - Going back: "↩ Back to page 13" appears at the bottom, next to the
    page number. Click it, or press Backspace or Alt+←, to go back to the
    page where you followed the link. It goes back step by step after
    several links, and disappears when you are back.
  - In Look-up mode a click looks up the word, even on a link.
  - "🔗 Page links" in the tools bar, or L, switches links off (every
    click turns the page) and on again. The choice is remembered.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  6. TABLE OF CONTENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Click "Contents" (or press C) to open the sidebar.

  - If the PDF has its own outline, it is shown right away.
  - Flipbook also finds chapters itself by reading the pages in the
    background and spotting headings. The progress shows at the top of the
    sidebar ("Finding chapters... page X of Y"). You can keep reading while
    it works; "Stop" pauses it and "Resume" continues later.
  - Scanned books with no text layer are read with built-in text recognition
    (OCR). This is slower: roughly a few seconds per page.
  - Results are saved, so each book is only scanned once. Large books may take
    a while the first time; progress is kept if you close the app.
  - Click any entry to go straight to that page. The entry for the part you
    are reading is highlighted.
  - Type in "Search contents..." to filter the list.
  - Detected titles from old scans can contain OCR spelling mistakes; the
    page numbers are still correct.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  7. BOOKMARKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Add in a colour: click "Bookmark" in the tools bar. A small palette
    opens: "Bookmark page N in:" with 8 colours. Click one, and the page is
    bookmarked with a ribbon in that colour.
  - Add quickly: press B, click "+ Bookmark page N" in the sidebar's
    Bookmarks tab, or click the ribbon spot at the top right of a page.
    These use the colour you chose last.
  - How to tell if a page is bookmarked (new in 1.7):
      Not bookmarked   Pointing at the page shows a faint ribbon outline.
                       Pointing at that ribbon makes it half-strength with
                       a "+" - click it to bookmark the page.
      Bookmarked       The ribbon is solid and fully coloured. When you add
                       it, the ribbon drops into place and a short note at
                       the top says "Page N bookmarked".
                       Pointing at a marked ribbon shows "✕" - click it to
                       remove the bookmark ("Bookmark on page N removed").
    The ribbon's tooltip also says "Page N is bookmarked" or
    "Bookmark this page".
  - Colours (new in 1.7.4): every bookmark keeps its own colour. The 8
    colours are Slate blue (the first default), Red, Orange, Gold, Green,
    Teal, Purple and Rose. All are mid-tone, readable on light and dark
    pages. Use them to group bookmarks, for example red for important
    passages and green for references.
      - Change a bookmark's colour: on that page, click "Bookmark"
        ("Bookmarked" when the page is marked) and pick another colour. Or
        click the colour dot next to the bookmark in the sidebar list.
      - The same palette has "✕ Remove bookmark".
      - Click outside the palette or press Esc to close it.
      - The colour you pick last is remembered (for every book) and used by
        B, a ribbon click and "+ Bookmark page N".
      - Bookmarks made before 1.7.4 keep the colour they were shown in.
  - Groups: the sidebar's Bookmarks tab lists bookmarks grouped by colour.
    Each colour has a heading with its count, for example "Red (3)", and the
    bookmarks under it are in page order.
  - Go to: open the sidebar (C), choose the Bookmarks tab, click a bookmark.
  - Rename: click the pencil (✎) next to a bookmark, type, press Enter
    (Esc cancels).
  - Remove: click the ✕ next to it, click the marked ribbon, press B again
    on a marked page, or use "✕ Remove bookmark" in the Bookmark palette.
  - Bookmarks are saved automatically and kept between sessions.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  8. READ ALOUD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Flipbook can read the book to you, sentence by sentence.

  Starting
  - Click "🔊 Read aloud" in the tools bar to show the Read aloud bar, then
    press ▶.
    Or simply press R (it opens the bar and starts reading).
  - Reading starts at the pages you are looking at - or, if you are on the
    page where you last stopped, at the exact sentence where you stopped.
  - To start at a chapter, click it in Contents first, then press ▶.
  - Read from here: while the Read aloud bar is open, click any sentence on
    the page and reading starts from that sentence. (Clicking the page margin
    still turns the page.)

  While it reads
  - The sentence being read is highlighted on the page. The highlight has a
    small margin around the text, so the first and last letters (and tall
    capitals) are always fully inside it, and a closing quote mark stays
    with the sentence it ends.
  - When it reaches the end of the pages you see, the book turns the page
    itself (with the page-flip animation) and keeps reading. A sentence that
    runs onto the next page turns the page partway through.
  - Running heads at the top of pages, page numbers and stray marks are
    skipped. Words split with a hyphen at a line end are joined again.
  - Chapter numbers, titles and section headings are read on their own,
    each ending like a sentence with a short pause, so "Chapter IV - The
    Early Years" is read "Chapter 4. The Early Years." and is not
    run into the first sentence after it. Flipbook spots a heading by its
    larger type, the space around it, or a short title line without a full
    stop. A small footnote number printed after a word or a title (as in
    "Years²⁴") is not read out.
  - Numbers are read as numbers: "4,000" is "four thousand", "1,500" is
    "one thousand five hundred", "3.5" is "three point five", and a year
    such as 1717 is "seventeen seventeen".
  - Dollar amounts are read as money: "$1,250.75" is "one thousand two
    hundred fifty dollars and seventy-five cents", "$1" is "one dollar",
    "$0.50" is "fifty cents" and "$4.5 million" is "four point five
    million dollars".

  If you turn the page by accident (or look ahead)
  - Reading PAUSES and keeps its place. The bar shows a button like
    "↩ p. 42".
  - Turn back to that page, or click "↩ p. 42", and reading continues with
    the same sentence - it does not start over.
  - Press ▶ instead if you want to read the new pages from where you are.
  - Prefer the old behaviour (reading jumps to whatever page you turn to)?
    Switch on "Follow my page turns" with the ⤳ button in the bar.

  The Read aloud bar
    ⏮  previous sentence (←)  ▶/⏸  play / pause (R or Space)
    ⏭  next sentence (→)      ⏹   stop

  Keys while the Read aloud bar is open: Space pauses and resumes (it
  starts reading if nothing is playing yet), and the Left / Right arrow
  keys go to the previous / next sentence while reading or paused. Page Up
  / Page Down still turn pages. Close the bar (✕) and Space and the arrow
  keys turn pages again as usual.
    Voice menu                choose a voice (see below and section 9). When
                              the list is open, the voice in use is
                              highlighted in blue and bold. Order: natural
                              AI voice, standard Windows voices, Qwen3-TTS
                              voices. It lists only voices: add or remove
                              voices in 🧩 Add-ons (tools bar).
    −  slider  +              reading speed
    ↩ p. N                    back to where reading paused (when you turned away)
    ⤳                         follow my page turns (on / off)
    Footnotes: skipped/read   read the footnotes at the bottom of pages or not
    CC                       caption: show the exact text being read
    Aa                        pronunciation fixes (section 11)
    ✕                         close the bar (stops reading)

  The caption (CC)
  - The text being read appears in its own box, just above the Read aloud
    bar. Before reading starts it says "The words being read appear here."
  - The book is made a little smaller to fit above the box, so the box never
    covers any words, footnotes or page numbers.
  - The box keeps the same size the whole time: a long sentence is shown in
    a slightly smaller font instead of a bigger box. Nothing on the screen
    moves while reading. Only turning CC on or off resizes the book.

  Footnotes (new in 1.7)
  - The button in the Read aloud bar always shows what will happen:
      "Footnotes: skipped"   (the default) footnotes at the bottom of a page
                             are not read; reading goes straight on to the
                             next page.
      "Footnotes: read"      (button highlighted) footnotes are read after
                             the page's main text.
    Click it to switch. Your choice is remembered. If you switch while it is
    reading, it carries on from the current sentence with the new setting.
  - How Flipbook finds footnotes: a block of smaller text at the bottom of
    the page that starts with a note mark - *, †, ‡, §, a number such as 1,
    (1) or [1], or a small raised number.
  - Limits: footnotes without a mark, or whose mark the text recognition
    of a scanned page misread, may not be found (then they are read).
    Smaller-type passages at the bottom of a page that do not start with a
    note mark (such as an editor's commentary) are read as normal text.
    Turn on CC to see exactly what is read.

  Moving around while it reads
  - ⏮ / ⏭ can be clicked several times quickly; only the sentence you land on
    is prepared, so the voice is not held up by the ones you skipped.
  - Sentences already read are kept ready, so going back plays at once.
  - Every sentence is spoken in one go with the natural AI voice, however
    long, so it never pauses in the middle as if a new sentence began. The
    next sentences are prepared while one is being read. Only the first
    sentence after ▶, a skip or a jump may be split once, at a comma or
    semicolon, so the voice starts sooner.
  - The next pages of scanned books are recognised ahead of time.

  Reading speed
  - From 0.5x (slow) to 2.0x (fast) in steps of 0.1; 1.0x is normal.
  - Use the slider, the − / + buttons, or the [ and ] keys. Click the speed
    label to go back to 1.0x.
  - The label shows the approximate pace, e.g. "1.3x · ~215 wpm".
  - With the AI voice a speed change applies at once, even mid-sentence.
    With standard Windows voices it applies from the next sentence.
  - Up to 1.3x the AI voice is spoken at that pace. Faster than that, it is
    spoken at 1.3x (where every consonant stays clear) and played back faster
    without changing the pitch.
  - Your speed and voice are remembered.

  Voices
  - Standard Windows voices (e.g. David, Zira, Mark) work right away with no
    download. They sound more mechanical.
  - The natural AI voice and the Qwen3-TTS voices are optional add-ons - see
    section 9.
  - Removing the standard Windows voices (new in 1.7.7): once you have the
    natural AI voice or the Qwen3-TTS voices, you can take the Windows
    voices out of the voice menu: in 🧩 Add-ons, click "Remove..." next to
    "Standard Windows voices". Windows itself keeps them; "Add back" (same
    place) brings them back.
  - At least one voice is always kept. With neither add-on voice installed
    (as for a new user), the Windows voices can't be removed: Add-ons says
    so. If you remove your last add-on voice, the Windows voices come back
    on their own.

  Scanned books
  - Pages without a text layer are read with text recognition (OCR) first,
    which takes a few seconds per page ("Recognizing text on page N...").
  - Old scans can produce recognition mistakes, and those mistakes are read
    out as they are. Turn on CC to see exactly what is being read; Pages with
    a real text layer read cleanly.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  9. THE NATURAL AI VOICE, THE GRAPHICS CARD AND QWEN3-TTS (OPTIONAL ADD-ONS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A high-quality, human-sounding voice with natural pacing and intonation.
  It is not included in Flipbook.exe, to keep the app small. It is downloaded
  only if you ask for it.

  Getting it
  1. Click "🧩 Add-ons" in the tools bar (or at the bottom of Home)
     and press "Add..." next to "Natural AI voice".
  2. The Add-ons list shows each add-on's state: "✓ Added", "Not added" or
     "Downloading NN%". It is in two groups:
       READ ALOUD VOICES   Natural AI voice, Qwen3-TTS voices and Standard
                           Windows voices. Each has a "Runs on" line at its
                           bottom: the natural AI voice lets you choose
                           (Automatic, graphics card or processor); Qwen3-TTS
                           runs on the graphics card only and says whether
                           it is loaded; the Windows voices run on the
                           processor.
       WORD LOOK-UP        the Etymology dictionary and the Legal
                           dictionary, each with its free download and
                           your own dictionaries (section 10).
  3. A notice explains the download:
       - Download: about 206 MB, one time, needs internet.
       - Adds about 206 MB of disk space, in the addons\voice folder next to
         Flipbook.exe.
       - After it's installed it works fully offline - no account, no cost,
         and your books never leave your PC.
     Choose Download, or Back.
  4. A progress bar shows the download. You can click "Hide" and keep
     reading; the progress then shows in the Read aloud bar.
     "Cancel download" stops it; "Try again" later continues where it
     stopped instead of starting over.
  5. Each file is checked against a fixed checksum. A damaged or altered
     download is deleted and never used.
  6. When it's done, Flipbook switches to the AI voice automatically.

  Voices included (choose in the voice menu)
    Heart (recommended), Bella, Nicole, Sarah  - American women
    Michael, Fenrir, Puck                      - American men
    Emma, Isabella                             - British women
    George, Fable, Lewis                       - British men

  Speed
  - On a typical PC the voice is prepared 3 to 4 times faster than it is
    spoken, a few sentences ahead, so reading flows without pauses. On older,
    slower computers there may be a short wait before some sentences.
  - With a suitable graphics card it can be made several times faster still
    (see "On the graphics card" below).

  On the graphics card (new in 1.4)
  Each time Flipbook starts, it checks your graphics card. If the card can
  make the voice (a real graphics card with at least 2 GB of its own video
  memory, any make: NVIDIA, AMD or Intel), Flipbook offers the "graphics-card
  speed-up" and then decides by itself where the voice is made.
  - Getting it: "🧩 Add-ons" > Natural AI voice > "Add the graphics-card
    speed-up (326 MB)...". When you add the AI voice itself on such a PC, a
    tick box adds the speed-up at the same time.
      - Download: about 326 MB, one time: the same Kokoro voice in a form a
        graphics card can run. Flipbook prepares it on your PC (a few
        seconds) in addons\voice\gpu; it then uses about 326 MB.
  - The self-test: after it is added, and again whenever the graphics card,
    its driver or Flipbook changes, Flipbook makes two test sentences on the
    graphics card and on the processor. The graphics card is used only if
    its voice matches the processor's and it is clearly faster. (On an
    NVIDIA RTX 3070 laptop: about 4-6x faster than the processor; the voice
    is prepared about 13 times faster than it is spoken.)
  - "Runs on" (in 🧩 Add-ons, under Natural AI voice):
      Automatic (recommended)  Flipbook decides, as described above.
      Graphics card (GPU)      always the graphics card, when it can be used.
      Processor (CPU)          always the processor - choose this if the
                               voice ever sounds wrong or the PC misbehaves.
    The line under it says which one is in use now and why. "Test again"
    repeats the self-test.
  - If the graphics card fails while reading (for example after a driver
    crash or update), the sentence is made on the processor instead and
    reading carries on; the Read aloud bar says so. Automatic then stays on
    the processor until you press "Test again" (or choose Graphics card).
  - The graphics card works in a separate helper program
    (Flipbook.exe --voice-worker), so a graphics-card problem can't close
    Flipbook. It ends when Flipbook ends.
  - Removing it: "Remove" next to "Graphics-card speed-up" (the voice keeps
    working on the processor).

  Removing it
  - "🧩 Add-ons" > "Remove..." next to "Natural AI voice", then confirm.
    Read aloud goes back to the standard Windows voice. You can download the
    AI voice again at any time.

  Computers without internet
  - Download the add-on on another PC, then copy its whole "addons\voice"
    folder into the folder that holds Flipbook.exe on the offline PC.
    (Files: addons\voice\kokoro-v1.0.fp16.onnx and voices-v1.0.bin)

    kokoro-onnx project's release page on GitHub:
    https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx/releases/tag/model-files-v1.0
    kokoro-v1.0.fp16.onnx  177,464,787 bytes  SHA-256
      C1610A859F3BDEA01107E73E50100685AF38FFF88F5CD8E5C56DF109EC880204
    voices-v1.0.bin         28,214,398 bytes  SHA-256
      BCA610B8308E8D99F32E6FE4197E7EC01679264EFED0CAC9140FE9C29F1FBF7D

    kokoro-v1.0.onnx       325,532,387 bytes  SHA-256

  Qwen3-TTS voices (new in 1.4, NVIDIA graphics cards only)
  A higher-end choice: very natural, expressive voices made on the graphics
  card by Alibaba's open Qwen3-TTS model (the 0.6-billion-parameter
  "CustomVoice" model). They are offered only if your PC can run them:
  an NVIDIA RTX 30-series or newer graphics card with at least 6 GB of
  video memory and a recent driver. Otherwise "🧩 Add-ons" shows
  "Not available on this PC" and says why.
  - Getting them: "🧩 Add-ons" > "Add..." next to "Qwen3-TTS voices".
      - Download: about 5.4 GB, one time, needs internet. It holds the voice
        model (2.5 GB) and its own private copy of Python with the PyTorch
        graphics-card (CUDA) libraries - nothing is installed into Windows.
      - Uses about 7.4 GB of disk space, in the addons\qwen folder.
      - Every file is checked against a fixed checksum; "Cancel" stops it
        and adding it again continues where it stopped.
      - The download and install take several minutes; you can keep
        reading meanwhile. The progress shows in the Add-ons list.
  - Using them: in the Read aloud bar's voice menu, under "Qwen3-TTS voices
    (graphics card)": Ryan and Aiden (the model's two English voices).
    They appear there as soon as the install finishes, even if you closed
    the Add-ons window meanwhile, and the Read aloud bar says "Qwen3-TTS
    voices installed ✓". No restart is needed.
  - The first time you choose one after starting Flipbook, it takes about
    20 seconds to load onto the graphics card. After that it makes speech
    about twice as fast as it is spoken (on an RTX 3070 laptop), a few
    sentences ahead.
  - Speed: these voices have no speed setting of their own, so the speed
    slider plays them faster or slower (the pitch is kept). The natural AI
    voice sounds better at very fast speeds.
  - They run in their own helper program, so a problem there can't close
    Flipbook; if it stops, the next sentence starts it again.
  - Removing them: "🧩 Add-ons" > "Remove..." next to "Qwen3-TTS voices".
  - Where they come from: Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-0.6B-CustomVoice (Apache 2.0,
    Qwen team, Alibaba Cloud) from Hugging Face, run with faster-qwen3-tts
    (MIT) and PyTorch (BSD) - the exact files and checksums are listed inside
    Flipbook (qwen.py in the source).

 10. WORD LOOK-UP AND THE DICTIONARIES (OPTIONAL ADD-ONS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Find out what a word means and where it comes from.

  Looking up a word
  - Right-click any word on the page, or
  - turn on Look-up mode with "🔍 Look up words" in the tools bar (or
    press D) and click words. In Look-up mode clicks define words instead of turning pages; use
    the arrows or keys to turn pages. Press D again to turn it off.
  - The word is outlined on the page and a card opens next to it.
  - Close the card with ✕, Esc, or by clicking elsewhere.

  What the card shows
  - The word and its pronunciation, with 🔊 to hear it in your Read aloud
    voice.
  - Meanings, grouped by noun / verb / adjective / adverb, with an example
    and similar words (from Open English WordNet - modern usage).
  - ETYMOLOGY - where the word comes from, e.g. for "candidate":
    "L. Candidatus ... (because candidates for office in Rome were clothed
    in a white toga.) fr. candidatus clothed in white".
  - Webster's 1913 definitions - often the sense the word had when older
    books were written.
  - Word forms are understood: "travelled" shows "travel", "libraries"
    shows "library".
  - Misread words from old scans get suggestions: "efiect" offers "effect",
    "rnodern" offers "modern". Click a suggestion to look it up.
  - "Aa" opens the pronunciation fixes for that word (section 11).
  - "More on Wiktionary ↗" opens the word on Wiktionary in your web browser.
    Only this link uses the internet, and only when you click it.

  The two dictionary boxes in Add-ons
  - "🧩 Add-ons" (tools bar, or the bottom of Home) has two boxes under
    "🔍 Word look-up". Each holds a free download AND the dictionaries you
    add from your own PDFs of that kind:
       Etymology dictionary   the free Webster's 1913 and WordNet, and your
                              own etymology or general dictionaries.
                              "Add a dictionary..." adds one of yours.
       Legal dictionary       the free Black's Law Dictionary, 2nd edition,
                              and your own law dictionaries (Black's 4th or
                              5th edition, or any other). "Add or change..."
                              opens the Legal dictionaries window.
    Your own dictionaries are listed inside their box, each with "Rename",
    "Move to ⚖ Legal" (or "Move to Etymology") and "Remove".

  Getting the free etymology dictionary (Webster's 1913 and WordNet)
  - "🧩 Add-ons" > "Etymology dictionary" > "Download..." next to
    "Webster's 1913 and WordNet". (The first time you look up a word, the
    card also offers it.) A notice explains it:
       - Download: about 40 MB, one time, needs internet, from Project
         Gutenberg and the Open English WordNet project.
       - Flipbook then builds the dictionary on your PC (about half a minute)
         in the addons\dictionary folder (about 46 MB).
       - Afterwards it works fully offline; nothing you look up is sent
         anywhere.
  - A progress bar shows the download and the building. "Cancel" stops it;
    trying again continues where it stopped. Downloads are checked against
    fixed checksums.
  - When it is ready, Look-up mode switches on.

  Removing it
  - "🧩 Add-ons" > "Etymology dictionary" > "Remove..." next to
    "Webster's 1913 and WordNet", then confirm. Your own etymology
    dictionaries stay.

  Computers without internet
  - Build it on another PC, then copy the whole "addons\dictionary" folder
    next to Flipbook.exe on the offline PC (the file inside is
    dictionary.sqlite).

  Legal or Etymology: the switch under "🔍 Look up words"
  - Right under "🔍 Look up words" in the tools bar is a small switch:
    "Etymology | ⚖ Legal". It sets which dictionary a look-up uses, and is
    remembered. In icons-only view it is one small button ("Ety" or "⚖");
    click it to switch.
       Etymology   the general dictionary described above (meanings and
                   word origins), with any general dictionary you added
                   from your own PDFs shown first.
       ⚖ Legal     Black's Law Dictionary and any law dictionary you added
                   from your own PDFs, for law books such as Coke's
                   "Institutes of the Lawes of England" or Blackstone.
  - With ⚖ Legal, a word is looked up in every edition of Black's you have
    added, newest first (5th, 4th, 2nd), so you can compare them, then in
    your own law dictionaries in the order you added them. Under
    the definitions are related legal terms (for "fee": "Fee simple",
    "Fee tail", ...; click one to look it up) and the word's etymology
    from the general dictionary, if that is added too.
  - A word that has no legal definition in any of them is shown from the
    general (etymology) dictionary instead, with a note saying so.
  - "General dictionary for ..." at the bottom of a legal card shows the
    word's everyday meaning.

  Getting the Legal dictionary
  - "🧩 Add-ons" > "Legal dictionary": "Download..." next to Black's 2nd
    edition gets the free one, and "Add..." / "Add or change..." opens the
    Legal dictionaries window (setting the switch to ⚖ Legal and
    right-clicking a word offers it too). The window has two steps:
    1. The free ones. "Get the free ones" downloads, one time:
       - Black's Law Dictionary, 2nd edition (1910), public domain, about
         7 MB from archive.org, and
       - the free etymology dictionary (Webster's 1913 and WordNet, about
         40 MB), if it isn't added yet. It answers when a word has no legal
         meaning, and adds word origins.
       One progress window shows both.
       If one of archive.org's servers has an expired security
       certificate, Flipbook tries another (up to 20 times) and a small
       note in the window says so. It never skips the security check; if
       none answers safely, try again later.
    2. Your own law dictionaries (optional): "Add another law
       dictionary..." for any law dictionary you have in PDF: Black's Law
       4th or 5th edition, Bouvier, Ballentine, Osborn or any other (see "A
       dictionary of your own" below).
       - Black's 4th and 5th are recognised from their title pages and read
         with Flipbook's own Black's reader, which is made for them. Choose
         the folder holding one edition's PDF files (or pick all its files
         together), or a folder that holds both editions' folders: then
         both are added, one after the other. It takes a few minutes for a
         whole edition; they are kept in the addons\legal folder next to
         Flipbook.exe.
  - The PDFs need a text layer (text you can select), not just scanned
    images. Your copies stay on this PC: they are never uploaded, and
    nothing you look up is sent anywhere.
  - Each edition can be removed on its own: "🧩 Add-ons" > "Remove" next to
    it. Your own PDF files are never touched.

  A dictionary of your own (PDF)
  - In "🧩 Add-ons": "Etymology dictionary" > "Add a dictionary..." for an
    etymology or general dictionary, or "Legal dictionary" > "Add or
    change..." > "Add another law dictionary..." for a law dictionary.
    Choose "PDF files..." for one PDF or the volumes of one dictionary
    (hold Ctrl to pick several), or "A folder..." for a dictionary split
    into files, such as one PDF per letter.
  - Flipbook suggests a name (you can change it). The box you add it from
    sets how it is used:
       Legal dictionary       shown when the switch is on ⚖ Legal, after
                              Black's Law.
       Etymology dictionary   shown when the switch is on Etymology, above
                              Webster's.
    "Add it" reads the dictionary once (a few minutes for a big book; you
    can go on reading meanwhile) and keeps its entries in the
    addons\mydicts folder next to Flipbook.exe. The PDF is not copied or
    changed, and nothing is sent anywhere.
  - Flipbook works out by itself how the book marks its entries: headwords
    in bold, in CAPITALS, or sticking out to the left, and it checks that
    they run in alphabetical order. A PDF that isn't a dictionary (a book
    or an article) is refused with a message.
  - In "🧩 Add-ons" each one is listed in its box with "Rename", "Move to
    ⚖ Legal" (or "Move to Etymology") and "Remove". Up to 20 can be added.

  Wrong dictionary? A warning
  - Flipbook checks whether a dictionary you add looks like the kind of the
    box you add it from. It only warns you; it never stops you.
  - Before reading: if the PDF's title or first pages clearly point to the
    other kind (for example a law dictionary added to the Etymology
    dictionary), it asks: "Add as Legal" (or "Add as Etymology"), "Keep as
    ..." or "Cancel". Black's Law 4th and 5th are always recognised.
  - After reading: if the words found in it clearly point to the other
    kind (many legal terms, or hardly any, among its headwords), the
    "Dictionary added" window asks whether to move it to the other box:
    "Move to ..." or "Keep in ...". If you keep it there, Add-ons shows
    "⚠ looks like ..." next to it.
  - Books that mix both (a general dictionary with many law words, a
    book of legal maxims) are left alone when the signs aren't clear. You
    can always move a dictionary to the other box later.

  Good to know
  - A dictionary of your own is read without knowing its layout, so some
    entries may be cut short, run into the next one, or be missing,
    especially in old scans or unusual layouts. A scan with no text in it
    (pictures of pages only) can't be read; run it through text
    recognition (OCR) first.
  - Black's 2nd edition comes from an old scan read by text recognition,
    so a few entries have misread letters or two entries run together.
  - Old spellings (as in Coke: "lawes", "seisin") are found only when
    Black's lists that spelling.
  - Some Greek words in Webster's etymologies were lost in the digital
    edition used, so a few etymologies are shortened (e.g. "Gr." with the
    Greek word missing). The Wiktionary link helps for those.
  - Some names and specialist terms are missing (common ones such as
    Minerva, Saturn and Jupiter are included); use the Wiktionary
    link for the rest.

  Where it comes from
  - Webster's Revised Unabridged Dictionary (1913), public domain,
    Project Gutenberg eBook #29765:
    https://www.gutenberg.org/files/29765/29765-0.txt

 11. PRONUNCIATION FIXES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  If Read aloud says a word wrongly, you can tell it how the word sounds.

  - Click "Aa" in the Read aloud bar (or "Aa" on a word's look-up card).
  - Type the word, then spell it the way it is said, using ordinary letters,
    e.g.  Worcestershire  ->  wuss ter sher
  - Press ▶ next to it to listen; "Add" saves it; ✕ deletes it.
  - Fixes apply to every book and to both kinds of voice, from the next
    sentence on.
  - Built in already: old spellings such as to-day, to-morrow, shew, shewn,
    connexion, mediaeval, encyclopaedia are read the modern way, and printed
    ligatures (ﬀ ﬁ ﬂ) are read as normal letters.
  - Tip: turn on CC first to see exactly what text the voice was given. If
    the caption shows a misread word (e.g. "efiect"), the problem is the old
    scan, not the voice.

 14. WHERE YOUR DATA IS STORED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  In this folder:
    flipbook-data\         Bookmarks, table-of-contents results (one small
                           file per book) and your pronunciation fixes.
                           Back this folder up to keep them.
                           voice-device.json in it holds the "Runs on"
                           choice and the last graphics-card test.
    addons\voice\          The natural AI voice, if you downloaded it
                           (addons\voice\gpu\: its graphics-card speed-up).
    addons\dictionary\     The free etymology dictionary (Webster's 1913
                           and WordNet), if you downloaded it.
    addons\legal\          Black's Law Dictionary, if you added it (built
                           from the 2nd edition and your own 4th / 5th).
    addons\mydicts\        Dictionaries you added from your own PDFs
                           (mydicts.sqlite: their entries only, not the
                           PDFs).

  In your Windows user profile:
    %LOCALAPPDATA%\Flipbook\WebView\    Last page read and last sentence
                                        read aloud per book, theme, sidebar,
                                        tools bar view (names / icons only),
                                        whether the first-run tip was seen,
                                        Read aloud voice, speed, caption,
                                        follow and footnotes settings, and
                                        the bookmark colour chosen last
                                        (each bookmark's own colour is saved
                                        with the bookmark in flipbook-data\).
    %LOCALAPPDATA%\Flipbook\error.log   Written only if something goes wrong.

  Note: bookmarks and contents of every book are saved in flipbook-data\,
  however the book was opened. Since 1.5, books opened in the Flipbook app
  (the Library, "Open a PDF...", dragging, "Open with") are also on your
  book list. (When Flipbook runs in a web browser via "Open Flipbook.bat",
  a PDF picked with "Open a PDF..." can't be added to the list, because the
  browser doesn't tell Flipbook where the file is.)
  books.json in flipbook-data\ is the book list: each book's location, the
  page you got to and when you read or added it.

  Books are recognised by their content, not their file name or location,
  so renaming or moving a PDF keeps its bookmarks and contents.

  Flipbook never changes, copies or uploads your PDFs. Read aloud and word
  look-up run on your PC; no text, words or audio are sent anywhere. Flipbook
  only uses the internet to download an add-on, and only when you ask it to
  (and the Wiktionary link opens your browser only when you click it).

 15. MOVING, COPYING OR UPDATING FLIPBOOK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Move or copy the whole folder. Keep Flipbook.exe, flipbook-data\ and
    addons\ together to keep your bookmarks, contents, pronunciation fixes
    and add-ons.
  - Shortcuts you made point at the old location; delete and recreate them
    after moving.
  - On another PC, books open at page 1 the first time (the last-page memory
    lives in the user profile, see section 14); bookmarks and contents saved
    in flipbook-data\ come along with the folder.

  Updating from an earlier version (1.0.0 - 1.4.0): close Flipbook, replace
  Flipbook.exe with the new one and keep everything else. Your bookmarks,
  contents, settings and add-ons carry over. The book list starts with the
  PDFs in this folder and fills up as you open books.

  Uninstalling: close Flipbook and delete this folder. To remove everything,
  also delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\Flipbook and any shortcuts you made. Nothing is
  installed into Windows.

 16. TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  "Windows protected your PC" on first start
     Click "More info" > "Run anyway" (see section 3).

  Message that WebView2 Runtime is required
     Install it from https://developer.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/webview2/
     (choose "Evergreen Bootstrapper"), then start Flipbook again.

  Nothing happens when I double-click, or the window is blank
     Wait a few seconds; the first start unpacks the app and is slower.
     Check Task Manager for a stuck "Flipbook" and end it, then try again.
     Look in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Flipbook\error.log for details.

  A book doesn't appear in the Library
     Open it once ("📂 Open a PDF..." on Home, or drag it in), or add it with
     "＋ Add PDFs to your library..." - from then on it stays on the list. If
     you removed it from the list earlier, opening it again puts it back.

  A book on the list says "File not found"
     The PDF was moved, renamed or deleted. Open it from its new place (it
     is listed again, with its bookmarks) and remove the old entry with ✕.

  "Continue reading" isn't shown when Flipbook starts
     It appears once you have opened a book in the app. If Flipbook was
     started by opening a PDF ("Open with" or dragging onto Flipbook.exe),
     that PDF opens directly instead; click "Library" to see your books.

  Where are the Add-ons?
     On Home (bottom left, "🧩 Add-ons") and, while reading, in the tools bar
     on the right. They are not in the Library, which only holds your books.

  Keyboard shortcuts don't work
     Click on the book once, and make sure the cursor isn't in a text box
     (for example the Page box in the tools bar).

  I can't find a button that used to be in the top bar
     Since 1.3.0, Go to page, Look up, Read aloud, Bookmark, theme and Full
     screen are in the tools bar on the right. If it shows icons only, hover
     over an icon to see its name, or click » / « at the top to show names.

  The pages look small
     Both sidebars take space. Close the Contents sidebar (C), or switch the
     tools bar to icons only (» at its top).

  Clicking the page doesn't turn it
     Look-up mode (🔍) is on, or Read aloud is open and you clicked text.
     Press D to leave Look-up mode, click the page margin, or use the arrows.

  The table of contents is empty or incomplete
     The background scan may still be running - see the progress at the top
     of the sidebar, and press Resume if it was stopped. Books without clear
     headings may produce few entries.

  Read aloud makes no sound
     Check the Windows volume and output device. Make sure a voice is
     selected in the voice menu. Press ⏹ and then ▶ to start again.

  Read aloud stopped after I turned the page
     That is on purpose: it paused to keep your place (section 8). Turn back,
     click "↩ p. N", or press ▶ to read the pages you are on now. Turn on ⤳ if
     you'd rather have reading follow your page turns.

  Read aloud reads the footnotes (or skips them) and I want the opposite
     Look at the "Footnotes:" button in the Read aloud bar and click it to
     switch between "skipped" and "read" (section 8). If footnotes are still
     read while it says "skipped", that page's notes have no mark Flipbook
     can recognise (common on poor scans).

  I can't tell whether a page is bookmarked
     A bookmarked page has a solid, fully coloured ribbon at its top right
     corner; a faint outline only means "click here to bookmark". The
     Bookmarks tab of the sidebar lists every bookmark (section 7).

  Read aloud says "Finding the text..." or "Recognizing text..." for a while
     The page is a scan and is being read with OCR first (a few seconds per
     page). Pages that are pictures only have nothing to read and are skipped.

  A word is read wrongly
     Turn on CC to see the text being read. If the text is right, add a
     pronunciation fix (section 11). If the text itself is wrong, the scan
     was misread; there is nothing the voice can do about that.

  The AI voice pauses between sentences
     The computer is preparing speech slower than it is spoken. Close other
     heavy programs, let the table-of-contents scan finish, or use a slightly
     faster reading speed. If your PC has a graphics card, add the
     graphics-card speed-up (section 9).

  "Graphics card failed ... the processor is used" / reading moved to the
  processor
     The graphics card or its driver had a problem, so Flipbook switched to
     the processor to keep reading. Restarting the PC or updating the graphics
     driver usually helps; then press "Test again" under 🧩 Add-ons > Natural
     AI voice > Runs on.

  The AI voice sounds different or wrong on the graphics card
     Choose "Processor (CPU)" under 🧩 Add-ons > Natural AI voice > Runs on.
     Flipbook remembers the choice.

  The Qwen3-TTS voices say "Not available on this PC"
     They need an NVIDIA RTX 30-series or newer graphics card with at least
     6 GB of video memory; the reason shown tells you what is missing (for
     example an old driver - update it from nvidia.com and restart Flipbook).

  A Qwen3-TTS voice takes a long time before it starts
     The first sentence after starting Flipbook loads the voice onto the
     graphics card (about 20 seconds; longer on the very first run). Keep the
     laptop plugged in: on battery the graphics card is much slower.

  The graphics-card speed-up isn't offered
     The line under "Runs on" says why: for example no graphics card was
     found, or it has less than 2 GB of its own video memory (built-in
     laptop graphics usually doesn't). The voice works on the processor.

  An add-on download fails or stops
     Check the internet connection and choose "Try again" - it continues
     where it stopped. "Can't write to the Flipbook folder" means the folder
     is read-only (e.g. in Program Files); move Flipbook to Documents.
     If a checksum fails repeatedly, the source file may have been changed
     upstream; please report it. Offline PCs: see sections 9 and 10.

  Last-read page, theme or reading voice was forgotten
     Flipbook normally uses one fixed local address. If another program (or a
     second Flipbook window) was using it, Flipbook picks the next one and
     these settings start fresh for that window. Use one Flipbook window at a
     time to avoid this.

  Antivirus warns about Flipbook.exe
     Unsigned apps packaged with PyInstaller are sometimes flagged by mistake.
     You can check that your copy is genuine with the file "Flipbook -
     SHA-256 checksum.txt" (section 17).

  Firewall prompt
     Flipbook's reader only talks to itself on 127.0.0.1 (this computer). It
     needs no incoming network access; it is safe to deny any prompt.

  Checksum           The exe's SHA-256 checksum and exact size are in a

  Built with         Python 3.13, PyInstaller 6.22 (single-file, windowed)
  Window             pywebview 6.2 on Microsoft Edge WebView2
  How it works       Flipbook.exe starts a small private web server on
                     127.0.0.1 (port 47863, or the next free one) that only
                     this computer can reach, and shows the reader in its own
                     window. The server reads PDFs in pieces, so even very
                     large books (6,000+ pages, hundreds of MB) open quickly.
                     Requests that change anything must come from the
                     Flipbook page itself; other web pages are refused.
  Security           (1.8.6) The server answers only requests addressed
                     to 127.0.0.1 or localhost on its own port, so a
                     website can't reach it by pointing its own name at
                     this computer ("DNS rebinding"). The reader page may
                     only talk to Flipbook itself and may not turn text
                     into program code (a Content Security Policy).
                     Since 1.8.7 the PDF engine is PDF.js 6.5, which no
                     longer contains the code behind CVE-2024-4367 (a
                     crafted PDF font running script) at all; the policy
                     stays as a second layer. Book paths sent from the page
                     must be local: network paths (\\server\share) are
                     ignored there, because even looking one up makes
                     Windows send your login to that server. (The Open
                     dialog and drag-and-drop still accept them.)
  PDF engine         PDF.js 6.5.438 (with character maps and standard fonts
                     for PDFs that don't embed their fonts, and its
                     WebAssembly decoders for JPEG 2000 and JBIG2 scanned
                     images and for colour profiles)
  Text recognition   Tesseract.js 5.1.1, English language data
  Read aloud         Standard voices: Windows speech (Web Speech API).
                     AI voice: Kokoro v1.0 (82M parameters, fp16 ONNX model)
                     run with ONNX Runtime 1.23 on the CPU, 24 kHz audio;
                     or, with the graphics-card speed-up, the fp32 model
                     adapted for DirectML (text part on the CPU, sound part
                     on the graphics card, in a helper process);
                     pronunciation by eSpeak NG (English data only). Speech is
                     made at most at 1.3x and time-stretched beyond that
                     (pitch kept). Requests carry a position number; the
                     voice engine drops outdated ones and serves the sentence
                     about to be spoken before ones prepared in advance.
  Qwen3-TTS voices   Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-0.6B-CustomVoice (bf16) with
                     faster-qwen3-tts (CUDA graphs) and PyTorch 2.10 + CUDA
                     12.8 in a private Python 3.12 in addons\qwen, run as a
                     helper process; 24 kHz audio.
  Etymology dict.    SQLite database built on your PC from Webster's 1913
                     and Open English WordNet 2025: about 189,000 headwords,
                     16,000 word forms; lookups take a few milliseconds.
